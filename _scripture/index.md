The confessions cite scripture; this is the other direction. The scripture proofs of the [Westminster Confession]({{ site.baseurl }}/pages/wcf/), the [Shorter]({{ site.baseurl }}/pages/wsc/) and [Larger]({{ site.baseurl }}/pages/wlc/) Catechisms, and the [Heidelberg Catechism]({{ site.baseurl }}/pages/heidelberg/) make **6,999 references** to **3,949 distinct passages** in **63 books** of the Bible. Pick a book to see which sections cite it.
