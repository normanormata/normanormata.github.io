## 1. Question: What is your only comfort in life and death?                                                                   

Answer: That I am not my own,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 1">1</a></sup> but belong with body and soul, both in life and in death,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 1">2</a></sup> to my faithful Saviour Jesus Christ.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 1">3</a></sup> He has fully paid for all my sins with His precious blood, and has set me free from all the power of the devil.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 5, question 1">5</a></sup> He also preserves me in such a way<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 6, question 1">6</a></sup> that without the will of my heavenly Father not a hair can fall from my head;<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 7, question 1">7</a></sup> indeed, all things must work together for my salvation.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 8, question 1">8</a></sup> Therefore, by His Holy Spirit He also assures me of eternal life<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 9, question 1">9</a></sup> and makes me heartily willing and ready from now on to live for Him.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q1-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 10, question 1">10</a></sup>

Answer: Christ teaches us this in a summary in Matthew 22: You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q4-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 4">1</a></sup> This is the great and first commandment. ## 6. Question: Did God, then, create man so wicked and perverse?

Answer: No, on the contrary, God created man good<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q6-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 6">1</a></sup> and in His image,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q6-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 6">2</a></sup> that is, in true righteousness and holiness,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q6-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 6">3</a></sup> so that he might rightly know God His Creator,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q6-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 4, question 6">4</a></sup> heartily love Him, and live with Him in eternal blessedness to praise and glorify Him.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q6-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 5, question 6">5</a></sup>

## 9. Question: Is God, then, not unjust by requiring in His law what man cannot do?

Answer: No, for God so created man that he was able to do it.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q9-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 9">1</a></sup> But man, at the instigation of the devil,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q9-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 9">2</a></sup> in deliberate disobedience<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q9-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 9">3</a></sup> robbed himself and all his descendants of these gifts.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q9-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 4, question 9">4</a></sup>

## 10. Question: Will God allow such disobedience and apostasy to go unpunished?

Answer: Certainly not. He is terribly displeased with our original sin as well as our actual sins. Therefore He will punish them by a just judgment both now and eternally,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q10-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 10">1</a></sup> as He has declared:<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q10-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 10">2</a></sup> Cursed be every one who does not abide by all things written in the book of the law, and do them (Galatians 3:10).

Answer: God is indeed merciful,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q11-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 11">1</a></sup> but He is also just.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q11-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 11">2</a></sup> His justice requires that sin committed against the most high majesty of God also be punished with the most severe, that is, with everlasting, punishment of body and soul.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q11-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 11">3</a></sup>

Answer: God demands that His justice be satisfied.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q12-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 12">1</a></sup> Therefore full payment must be made either by ourselves or by another.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q12-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 12">2</a></sup>

Answer: No. In the first place, God will not punish another creature for the sin which man has committed.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q14-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 14">1</a></sup> Furthermore, no mere creature can sustain the burden of God's eternal wrath against sin and deliver others from it.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q14-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 14">2</a></sup>

Answer: He must be a true man because the justice of God requires that the same human nature which has sinned should pay for sin.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q16-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 16">1</a></sup> He must be a righteous man because one who himself is a sinner cannot pay for others.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q16-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 16">2</a></sup>

## 21. Question: What is true faith?                                                                                                             

Answer: True faith is a sure knowledge whereby I accept as true all that God has revealed to us in His Word.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 21">1</a></sup> At the same time it is a firm confidence<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 21">2</a></sup> that not only to others, but also to me,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 21">3</a></sup> God has granted forgiveness of sins, everlasting righteousness, and salvation,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 4, question 21">4</a></sup> out of mere grace, only for the sake of Christ's merits.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 5, question 21">5</a></sup> This faith the Holy Spirit works in my heart by the gospel.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q21-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 6, question 21">6</a></sup>

## 23. Question: What are these articles?

Answer: III.1.I believe in God the Father almighty, III.1. Creator of heaven and earth. III.2.I believe in Jesus Christ, III.2.His only begotten Son, our Lord; III.3.He was conceived by the Holy Spirit, III.3.born of the virgin Mary; III.4.suffered under Pontius Pilate, III.4.was crucified, dead, and buried; III.4.He descended into hell; III.5.On the third day He arose from the dead; III.6.He ascended into heaven, III.6.and sits at the right hand III.6.of God the Father almighty; III.7.from there He will come to judge III.7.the living and the dead. III.8.I believe in the Holy Spirit; III.9.I believe a holy catholic Christian church, III.9.the communion of saints; III.10.the forgiveness of sins; III.11.the resurrection of the body; III.12.and the life everlasting.


## 24. Question: How are these articles divided?

Answer: Into three parts: the first is about God the Father and our creation; the second about God the Son and our redemption; the third about God the Holy Spirit and our sanctification.

## 80. Question: What difference is there between the Lord's supper and the papal mass?

Answer: The Lord's supper testifies to us, first, that we have complete forgiveness of all our sins through the one sacrifice of Jesus Christ, which He Himself accomplished on the cross once for all;<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q80-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 80">1</a></sup> and, second, that through the Holy Spirit we are grafted into Christ,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q80-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 80">2</a></sup> who with His true body is now in heaven at the right hand of the Father,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q80-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 80">3</a></sup> and this is where He wants to be worshipped.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q80-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 4, question 80">4</a></sup> But the mass teaches, first, that the living and the dead do not have forgiveness of sins through the suffering of Christ unless He is still offered for them daily by the priests; and, second, that Christ is bodily present in the form of bread and wine, and there is to be worshipped. Therefore the mass is basically nothing but a denial of the one sacrifice and suffering of Jesus Christ, and an accursed idolatry.

## 84. Question: How is the kingdom of heaven opened and closed by the preaching of the gospel?

Answer: According to the command of Christ, the kingdom of heaven is opened when it is proclaimed and publicly testified to each and every believer that God has really forgiven all their sins for the sake of Christ's merits, as often as they by true faith accept the promise of the gospel. ## 85. Question: How is the kingdom of heaven closed and opened by church discipline?

## 127. Question: What is the sixth petition?

Answer: And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one. That is: In ourselves we are so weak that we cannot stand even for a moment.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 127">1</a></sup> Moreover, our sworn enemies-- the devil,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 127">2</a></sup> the world,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 3, question 127">3</a></sup> and our own flesh<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 4, question 127">4</a></sup>-- do not cease to attack us. Wilt Thou, therefore, uphold and strengthen us by the power of Thy Holy Spirit, so that in this spiritual war<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 5, question 127">5</a></sup> we may not go down to defeat, but always firmly resist our enemies, until we finally obtain the complete victory.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q127-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 6, question 127">6</a></sup>

## 128. Question: How do you conclude your prayer?

Answer: For Thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. That is: All this we ask of Thee because, as our King, having power over all things, Thou art both willing and able to give us all that is good,<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q128-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 128">1</a></sup> and because not we but Thy holy Name should so receive all glory for ever.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q128-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 2, question 128">2</a></sup>

## 129. Question: What does the word Amen mean?

Answer: Amen means: It is true and certain. For God has much more certainly heard my prayer than I feel in my heart that I desire this of Him.<sup class="proof-marker"><a href="#hc-q129-proofs" aria-label="Scripture proof 1, question 129">1</a></sup>
